# Complaint Type Analysis

Using the command-line tool, I analyzed NYC 311 complaints during January and February 2024. After summing complaint counts across all boroughs, the most abundant complaint type was `HEAT/HOT WATER`, with 81,631 complaints across the two months.

The monthly counts were:

- January: 51,320
- February: 30,311
- June: 3,513
- July: 3,376

The number of `HEAT/HOT WATER` complaints was substantially higher during January and February than during June and July. January and February contained 81,631 complaints in total, compared with only 6,889 during June and July. This represents approximately a 91.6% decrease between the two periods.

The results show a strong seasonal difference in this complaint category, with far more HEAT/HOT WATER complaints occurring during the winter months than during the summer months.
